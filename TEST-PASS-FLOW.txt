v8 Test Pass flow:
- Student sees Test Pass on dashboard.
- Student opens Courses and enrolls in a course named "Test Pass".
- Enrollment is stored in enrolledCourses.
- Mock-test.html is locked until a Test Pass enrollment exists.
- After enrollment, Mock Tests open normally.
- My Course shows a "Give Mock Test" button for Test Pass.
- Matching is by title/name containing "test pass", or isTestPass=true, or accessType="test-pass".

For production multi-device support, mirror enrollments to Firestore under the authenticated
student UID rather than relying only on localStorage.
